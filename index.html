How it works · three primitives

Every answer is a distribution. Nothing is generated.

Rankine encodes the state once and keeps it in cache. Each question runs as a short suffix, and the answer comes straight from the logits over the option markers. The domain rules arrive inside the input, so one model covers every workflow.

choice

Pick one of up to 255 options

Routes a ticket, labels an incident, names the owner. Returns a probability for every option. Accuracy 0.750.

score

Grade against a rubric

A distribution over ordered levels plus an expected score between them. 97.1% of scores land within one level.

noul

Test a proposition

The probability a statement about the state holds. The strongest primitive at 0.830 accuracy.

Why Rankine exists · The problem

Every workflow generates. Few decide locally, with odds attached.

Teams route, grade and triage thousands of records a day. Most of those calls go to a hosted frontier model, arrive as a single label with no probability, and leave the building to get made.
